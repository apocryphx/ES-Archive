Settings

Choose ES Archive MCP ▸ Settings… (⌘,), or Settings… in the menu-bar status item.

Appearance

Two mutually exclusive ways for the app to present itself. Only one is ever active.

ModeWhat you get
Dock appA Dock icon and the full menu bar. Everything is reachable from the menus.
Menu bar onlyNo Dock icon, no app menus — just a status item in the menu bar carrying the same commands.

Pick Menu bar only if you want the archive available but out of the way. Everything you need — Archive Scope, Back Up, Restore, Personas, Connect, Settings, Quit — is in the status-item menu, along with a live entry count.

Applied to the next session

When your assistant spawned the running copy, it cannot relaunch itself without cutting that conversation off. Click Apply and the change is saved; it takes effect the next time a session starts. The current window keeps its present mode.

Startup

SettingEffect
Launch at loginStarts the app when you log in, so the archive's windows are at hand without waiting for an assistant to spawn it. Registered as a macOS login item — you can also see it in System Settings ▸ General ▸ Login Items.
Show main window at launchOpens the app's window automatically at startup. Turn it off to start straight to the menu bar.

Changes take effect when you click Apply. The button stays dimmed until something has actually changed.

The Connect window at startup

Help ▸ Connect ES Archive… opens by itself when you launch the app, which is useful until you have connected a client and unhelpful afterwards. Its own Show at next startup checkbox, at the bottom of that window, turns the behaviour off. The window stays available from the Help menu either way.

Personas

The author list has its own window — ES Archive MCP ▸ Manage Personas…. See Personas.
