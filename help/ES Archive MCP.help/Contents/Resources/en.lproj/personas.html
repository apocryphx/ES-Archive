Personas

Who wrote what, in one shared archive.

Every entry carries the name of the persona that wrote it. A persona is not an account and has no password — it is an authorship stamp, and it is what lets several assistants share this Mac without reading each other's notes. Each persona sees and writes only its own entries.

Any name that appears as an author in the archive is a persona. There is no separate list to maintain: a persona exists exactly as long as it has records. Archive Scope's persona picker lists all of them, plus All (witness) for the view across everything.

How the persona is chosen

Authorship travels with the connection. The connector this app builds for Claude Desktop launches it writing as Claude; another client can pass a different author name on its command line, and its records are stamped accordingly. Two assistants connected two different ways keep two cleanly separated slices of the same archive.

Manage Personas

Choose ES Archive MCP ▸ Manage Personas… (or the same item in the menu-bar status item). The window lists every author in the archive, with the number of records a delete or merge would touch — entries, their revisions, comments and attachments.

ButtonWhat it does

Merge…Folds its records into another persona, and removes it.
Deleting and merging cannot be undone

Deleting a persona destroys its entries along with it, and merging rewrites authorship on every record it touches. Take a backup first — File ▸ Back Up…. If you use iCloud, remember that the deletion syncs to your other Macs.
