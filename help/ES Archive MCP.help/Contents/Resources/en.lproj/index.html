ES Archive MCP Help

A memory for your AI assistant, kept on this Mac. Version 3.3.8.

Your assistant forgets everything when a conversation ends. ES Archive MCP gives it an archive that does not: it writes things down, finds them again months later, connects them, and lets go of what has gone stale — all in files that never leave your Mac unless you sign into iCloud.

Unlike a server you keep running, this app is spawned by the app you chat with: Claude Desktop or LM Studio starts it when a conversation needs the archive and speaks to it directly. You can also open it yourself, any time, to look at the archive.
