Personas

Who wrote what, in one shared archive.

Every entry carries the name of the persona that wrote it. A persona is not an account and has no password — it is an authorship stamp, and it is what lets several assistants share this Mac without reading each other's notes. Each persona sees and writes only its own entries.

Any name that appears as an author in the archive is a persona, including one that no client currently uses. Archive Scope's persona picker lists all of them, plus All (witness) for the view across everything.

How the persona is chosen

ES Archive Server binds one listening port per persona, and the port a request arrives on is its identity. The client never asserts who it is, so it cannot claim to be someone else, and names cannot drift. Point a client at port 3111 and it writes as whoever holds port 3111.

Settings ▸ Personas

The pane lists every persona in the archive with its record count.

ButtonWhat it does
+Creates a persona. The name is what gets stamped on its records.
–Deletes the persona and every record it wrote.
Rename…Re-stamps every one of its records under a new name.
Merge…Folds its records into another persona, and removes it.

Settings ▸ Ports

Each row binds a port to a persona. Add rows with +, remove them with –, then click Apply & Relaunch — port bindings only take effect when the server restarts.

Above the table, a separate checkbox switches the fixed-port Claude bridge on and off. Its port is not editable — the bridge hard-codes it — and stays reserved even while the bridge is off.

Reaching a persona from elsewhere

The server binds to 127.0.0.1 only and accepts nothing from outside this Mac. If you want a persona reachable remotely, put it behind a cloudflared tunnel of your own and turn on Require JWT for that port, so Cloudflare Access authenticates at the edge before anything reaches the archive. Because the setting is per port, one persona can be exposed while another stays strictly local.

Deleting and merging cannot be undone

Deleting a persona destroys its entries along with it, and merging rewrites authorship on every record it touches. Take a backup first — File ▸ Back Up…. If you use iCloud, remember that the deletion syncs to your other Macs.
