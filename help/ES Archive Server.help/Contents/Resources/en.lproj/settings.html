Settings

Choose ES Archive Server ▸ Settings… (⌘,). Three panes: UI Settings, Personas and Ports.

Appearance

Two mutually exclusive ways for the app to present itself. Only one is ever active.

ModeWhat you get
Dock appA Dock icon and the full menu bar. Everything is reachable from the menus.
Menu bar onlyNo Dock icon, no app menus — just a status item in the menu bar carrying the same commands.

Pick Menu bar only if you want the archive available but out of the way. Everything you need — Archive Scope, Back Up, Restore, Personas, Settings, Connect, Quit — is in the status-item menu.

Switching modes needs a relaunch

After you click Apply, the app offers to quit and reopen itself. Choose Later and the change waits until you next start it.

Startup

SettingEffect
Launch at loginStarts the server when you log in, so its ports are listening before any client asks. Registered as a macOS login item — you can also see it in System Settings ▸ General ▸ Login Items.
Show main window at launchOpens the Dashboard automatically at startup. Turn it off to start straight to the menu bar. You can always reach it later with Window ▸ Show Dashboard.

Changes take effect when you click Apply. The button stays dimmed until something has actually changed.

The Connect window at startup

Help ▸ Connect ES Archive… opens by itself when the app starts, which is useful until you have connected a client and unhelpful afterwards. Its own Show at next startup checkbox, at the bottom of that window, turns the behaviour off. The window stays available from the Help menu either way.
