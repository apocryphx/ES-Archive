ES Archive Server Help

A memory for your AI assistants, served from this Mac. Version 3.3.5.

Your assistant forgets everything when a conversation ends. ES Archive Server gives it an archive that does not: it writes things down, finds them again months later, connects them, and lets go of what has gone stale — all in files that never leave your Mac unless you sign into iCloud.
